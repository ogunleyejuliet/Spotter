# Spotter

Spotter is a gym membership app that helps members get answers about their gym, track attendance, view membership and payment records, and pay their subscriptions without relying on the front desk.

## The Problem

Gym staff spend a significant part of their day answering repetitive questions about class schedules, membership rules, access hours, attendance, and outstanding payments.

Some answers are easy to provide. Others require checking paper records, appointment books, or payment ledgers that may not be readily available.

Spotter gives members direct access to the information they need through one app.

## How It Works

Spotter retrieves answers from the gym's own records rather than the public internet.

It works with two categories of information:

- **Gym records:** Timetables, prices, access hours, guest policies, membership terms, and written training plans.
- **Member records:** Attendance history, membership tier, expiry date, payment history, and outstanding balance.

Members can ask questions in natural language and receive answers supported by the relevant record, along with the date it was last confirmed.

Personal records are restricted to the member they belong to.

## Core Features

- **AI-powered information retrieval:** Ask questions about gym policies, schedules, and membership information.
- **Record-backed answers:** View the source record supporting an answer and when it was last confirmed.
- **Attendance tracking:** Check in through the app and review attendance history.
- **Membership management:** View membership tier, expiry date, and payment records.
- **Subscription payments:** Pay subscriptions through a Nigerian payment gateway, with funds going directly to the gym's account.
- **Tier-based access:** Basic and Premium members receive access to different categories of gym information.

## Membership Tiers

**Basic**
- Timetables and class schedules
- Prices and membership policies
- Access hours and guest rules

**Premium**
- Everything included in Basic
- Written training plans
- Trainer guidance

## Product Scope

The first version focuses on information retrieval, attendance tracking, membership records, and subscription payments.

Automated renewal reminders, follow-ups, and agentic workflows are planned for a future version.

## Key Principles

- **Grounded answers:** Responses must be based on the gym's records.
- **Privacy:** Members can only access their own personal records.
- **Transparency:** Answers show the supporting record and its last-confirmed date.
- **Controlled access:** Information availability depends on membership tier.
- **Reliable payments:** The app facilitates payments without holding members' money.

## Project Status

Spotter is currently in the product definition and development preparation stage. The initial concept has been refined into a Product Requirements Document (PRD), with an `AGENTS.md` file created to provide project-specific instructions for AI-assisted development.

## Vision

Make gym information and membership records accessible to members whenever they need them, while reducing repetitive work at the front desk.
